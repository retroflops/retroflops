The Strong National Museum of Play inducted Space Invaders into its World Video
Game Hall of Fame with the class of 2016. The museum's record names Tomohiro
Nishikado as the designer and gives 1978 as the year Taito released the arcade
game in Japan. It credits that cabinet with making an on-screen high score a
standard part of an arcade game.

What the record says about this console is a claim about the hardware rather
than about the game. In 1980 Space Invaders became the 2600's most popular
title, and the museum's judgment is that millions of people bought the console
in order to shoot aliens at home.
