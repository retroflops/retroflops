---
title: The bundled game that moved the console
sourceIds:
  - strong-hall-of-fame-wii-sports
---

Wii Sports entered The Strong National Museum of Play's World Video Game Hall of
Fame with the class of 2023. It was released in 2006 and bundled with consoles
sold outside Japan, which is the fact the rest of the museum's record rests on.

The record describes a design that left things out on purpose. Its baseball was
pitching and hitting; a player never fielded a fly ball or ran the bases. The
museum borrows the game critic Jesper Juul's term "casual revolution" for this,
and argues that matching an arm swing to what happened on screen removed a
barrier for people who had no intention of memorizing a controller.

It then records where the game turned up. Senior centers ran bowling leagues on
it for people who had bowled decades earlier but found a real ball too heavy,
and in 2011 the health insurer Aetna sponsored a championship on its successor.
The museum puts the console above 100 million units sold and the game itself
above 82 million copies.
