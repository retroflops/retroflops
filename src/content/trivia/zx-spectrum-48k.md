The Science Museum Group holds a 48K Spectrum with its power supply and a
Horizon Software Starter Pack cassette, catalogued as object 1985-195 and dated
1982 to 1985.

The curatorial note attached to it states what the machine was for. Clive
Sinclair introduced the ZX Spectrum in August 1982, meaning it as an upgraded
ZX81 that could compete with the color graphics of the recently introduced
Atari, Commodore and Acorn computers.

The note ends with an assessment: the Spectrum was Sinclair Research's most
significant commercial success, and it is what gave the company market dominance
in the UK in the early 1980s.
