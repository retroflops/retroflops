title: A mascot built to beat Mario

Sonic the Hedgehog entered The Strong National Museum of Play's World Video Game
Hall of Fame with the class of 2016, and the museum's record is direct about why
the character exists at all. Sega wanted a rival to Nintendo's Mario and ran an
in-house design contest to get one. The winner was a hedgehog codenamed "Mr.
Needlemouse", colored cobalt blue and given large red shoes.

The record dates the game to 1991 and describes what the level design was for.
Slopes, springs and loops were arranged so that a player could reach the end
without slowing down, and the character died if a level took more than ten
minutes.

On sales the museum states more than 15 million copies, which makes it the
best-selling game for this console. It uses the North American name, Genesis;
this catalog files the machine under the name Sega sold it with in Europe and
Japan.
