---
title: Twenty-two million of one machine
sourceIds:
  - chm-timeline-1982
---

The Computer History Museum's timeline entry for 1982 records the Commodore 64
arriving at $595 with 64 KB of RAM. The same entry carries what happened next.
By the time the machine was discontinued in 1993 it had sold more than 22
million units, and thousands of software titles had been released across its
lifespan.

The museum also records who did the counting. It cites the 2006 Guinness Book of
World Records as recognizing the C64 as the greatest selling single computer of
all time. That is a records book adjudicating a manufacturer's own figures
rather than an audited count, which is why it is attributed here rather than
stated as a plain fact.
