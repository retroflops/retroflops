title: Where the three letters come from
sourceIds:

The Computer History Museum's timeline entry for 1999 is one of the places the
term "GPU" can be pinned to a product. The museum describes demand for video on
personal computers pushing toward a processor built specifically to handle
graphics, and names the result a Graphics Processing Unit.

The museum is careful with the ranking, and the hedge is worth repeating as a
hedge: this card is "often thought of" as the first consumer GPU. The entry adds
that it was expensive and sold extremely well anyway.

What the part was for is stated plainly. It took graphics calculations off the
central processor so that the processor could spend its time on everything else.
