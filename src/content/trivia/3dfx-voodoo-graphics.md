The Computer History Museum's timeline entry for 1996 records who started 3dfx:
three former Silicon Graphics employees, setting up to design chipsets for other
companies' graphics cards rather than to build cards themselves.

The museum's account of the company's early success points at arcades rather
than at home machines, naming San Francisco Rush and Wayne Gretzky's 3D Ice
Hockey among the cabinets built on the Voodoo system.

The same entry contains the end of the story, and it involves two machines that
also appear in this catalog. A partnership to supply graphics hardware for
Sega's Dreamcast was attempted and failed; better competing 3D cards arrived;
and the company eventually sold all of its intellectual property to Nvidia.
